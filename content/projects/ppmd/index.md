The *Party Policy in Modern Democracies* (PPMD) dataset contains expert survey scores of the policy positions of political parties in 47 countries. It accompanies the book of the same title, by Kenneth Benoit and Michael Laver (London: Routledge, 2006).

Data are available from the [project website](https://www.tcd.ie/Political_Science/ppmd/).
