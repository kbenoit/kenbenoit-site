### Other software

*   **Wordscores**: software for coding political texts, for Stata (no longer hosted).
*   **EzI: A(n Easy) Program for Ecological Inference**, with Gary King, 1996–2002. Computer software for MS-DOS, OS/2, and Windows 95, written in Gauss. Winner of the 1998 APSA Best Research Software Award. [gking.harvard.edu/ezi](https://gking.harvard.edu/ezi)

## Datasets

*   **Party Policy in Modern Democracies**, with Michael Laver, 2004–2005. Expert survey scores of policy positions of political parties in 47 countries. Accompanies the book of the same title. [Data and book website](https://www.tcd.ie/Political_Science/ppmd/)
*   **Record of American Democracy, 1984–1990**, with Gary King, Bradley Palmquist, Greg Adams, Micah Altman, Claudine Gay, Jeffrey B. Lewis, Kenneth R. Mayer, and Eric Reinhardt, 1999. Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research Number 2162. Winner of the 1999 APSA Best Research Web Site Award.
